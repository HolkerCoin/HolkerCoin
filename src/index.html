How it holds together

Easy to say, hard to build: keep a ledger useful on the days the everyday internet is not.

  1. Many paths, one ledger

    Messages can ride whichever link is still alive: long-range wireless, local meshes, plain broadband. No single carrier has to survive.

  2. A door, not a cage

    Everyday users get in through gateways on ordinary devices, while the core stays independent of any one provider.

  3. Nobody holds the off switch

    No cloud account, no carrier contract, no single operator whose bad day turns into yours.

  4. Start where the liquidity is

    Holker begins on Solana, so holders get fast settlement today while the sturdier network is built behind it.

Where it earns its keep

Plans look great until the thing they lean on disappears. These are the days we are designing for.

Grid failures

Substations trip and whole regions go dark. Equipment built to ride that out keeps the ledger reachable while the lights are off.

Switched-off networks

When a government or carrier pulls the plug, your balance should not become a hostage to their decision.

Storm season

Floods take out cables, wind takes out towers. Value should still be spendable after the weather has done its worst.

Resilience is cheap to promise and expensive to build. We would rather show the work.
